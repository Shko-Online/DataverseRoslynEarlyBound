## Derived work

### DLaB.Xrm.XrmToolBoxTools (Early Bound Generator)

<https://github.com/daryllabar/DLaB.Xrm.XrmToolBoxTools>

Generated code follows the conventions of Daryl LaBar's Early Bound Generator so that it can
replace code generated by that tool. To that end:

- `src/ShkoOnline.EarlyBound.Core/Naming/CamelCaser.cs` is a port of its `CamelCaser`.
- `src/ShkoOnline.EarlyBound.Core/Naming/NamingService.cs` follows the rules of its `NamingService`,
  including its table of global option set names.
- `src/ShkoOnline.EarlyBound.Core/Resources/CamelCaseDictionary.txt` is its English word list,
  unmodified.
- The default values of `tokenCapitalizationOverrides` and `optionNameOverrides` in
  `src/ShkoOnline.EarlyBound.Core/Configuration/GeneratorSettings.cs` are its defaults.
